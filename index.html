Game Rules

Master the hierarchy of Emperor, Citizen, and Slave

The Hierarchy

The game revolves around three main roles with a rock-paper-scissors dynamic: Emperor beats Citizen, Citizen beats Slave, but the Slave beats the Emperor!

Scoring Points

Standard victories grant +1 point. However, if the Slave manages to defeat the Emperor, it triggers a major upset granting a massive +5 points!

Camps & Groups

The match spans across 4 groups (totaling 12 rounds). Your deck alternates between playing as the powerful Emperor Camp or the underdog Slave Camp.

Persistence

Your progress, scores, and remaining cards are automatically saved in real-time. Feel free to refresh or leave; your game state will safely wait for you.
